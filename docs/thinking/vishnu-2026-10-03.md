This preserves the trajectory, not an invented transcript. The supplied conversation includes
many user turns, a user-pasted retrospective summary, recent assistant responses and the original
IDEA.md. Earlier assistant replies are missing. Entries below distinguish interests, revisions
and proposals rather than pretending every idea was accepted. Do not tidy this into a success

## 1. Start: infrastructure and a broad idea pool

The team considered Databento, Massive, TigerData, OCI/AWS/Render, HiPerGator and GPU credits.
It also explored Hyperliquid, compute pricing, satellite imagery, filings, energy/water, typed
language models, optimal transport and quantum algorithms. The common motivation was to build
something original connected to the AI economy, not another generic black-box return model.

Revision: having hardware, credits or an API does not establish an edge. Infrastructure serves
the research question; it is not the research question. The capture does not verify accounts or
credits mentioned in those discussions.

## 2. Physical bottlenecks became the economic thread

The original IDEA.md framed announced capacity as delivery claims and proposed regional EIA
generator schedule slippage, optimal transport, long constraint suppliers/short dependent names,
an options overlay and crypto monitoring.

What survives as a candidate: unexpected changes to delivery/capacity can affect firms through
contracts, margins, utilization, project timing and substitution. Original forecasts and later
revisions can make the phenomenon numeric rather than binary.

What is not established: that power is always the binding constraint; that the market prices
delivery as certain; that sparse physical files are unpriced; or that every supplier benefits
from scarcity while every developer loses. Physical scarcity can persist without financial
mispricing. A supplier can face cost inflation and a developer can pass costs through.

## 3. Satellite imagery and hearings were considered, then deprioritized

Construction imagery, permits, hearing video/transcripts and local approvals could reveal
milestones. The user recognized imagery is slow/noisy and asked for numeric alternatives.

Current proposal: start with dated disclosures and explicit quantities/dates. Permits and
imagery remain possible extensions only after demonstrating historical labels, release times,
entity matching and incremental information. A persuasive picture is not a testable time series.

## 4. Long price history was not enough

The user repeatedly pushed back on lists of APIs and twenty years of market prices: we also need
historical announcements, expectations and comparable operational fields. A recent GPU index or
perpetual cannot inherit a long validation record from unrelated gas futures or equities.

Revision: judge usable overlap, not the oldest date advertised by a provider. Price history,
operational-field continuity, known-at-time timestamps and exposure mapping must all intersect.
Historical analogues may test a general delivery mechanism; they do not prove an AI-specific
effect before that exposure existed.

## 5. Event count and regime count replaced calendar length as the sufficiency test

User-pasted context clarified the earthquake analogy: many years with few relevant shocks still
contain few independent observations. Thousands of filings can be repeated reports of the same
project or a common shock.

Preserve two different questions: chronological deployment validity and regime robustness.
Fit regime thresholds using earlier information. Study development-period chronological folds
for volatility/liquidity/demand differences. A retrospective balanced regime sample is
exploration, not automatically an executable backtest. A long period is a preference, not proof
of statistical adequacy. Do not force old incompatible measures into one series to reach ten years.

## 7. Quantify surprises, not merely events

The user asked for lead times, delivery promises, delays, compute prices and second-order
changes, not a yes/no classifier. Whiteboard interests: flow persistence, spread normalization,
volatility, selling acceleration/deceleration, tails and long/short risk balance.

Revision: operational signal inputs and execution inputs are different. Use filings to measure
changes against earlier expectations. Use quotes/trades initially to test execution, rather than
quietly adding a second order-flow alpha strategy. Backlog growth, a scheduled commencement lag
and a true unexpected delay are different objects.

## 10. Corrections to carry forward from the original IDEA.md

- Realized dates learned later are labels, not features at the earlier decision time. Revision
  signals can use two vintages already public; lifetime completion outcomes cannot.
- Generator IDs preserve project identity. A distribution distance can mask which project
  slipped. Start with matched-unit delays and cohort/censoring checks before transport methods.
- Operating-tab transitions are publication/reporting observations, not necessarily exact
  completion timestamps. Missing units are not automatically canceled; unequal mass needs an
  explicit policy. Technology MW is not equivalent firm capacity or data-center supply.
- A named region does not provide equity exposure weights. Establish historical assets,
  customers/contracts and regional exposure; otherwise label the mapping speculative.
- An 8-K deadline is not a pre-announced tradable event calendar. Pre-event chains selected using
  a future filing are retrospective event studies unless a valid ex-ante trading trigger exists.
- Compute futures listing, index methodology/history and Hyperliquid L4 availability in the old
  draft are not certified by this handoff. Verify each instrument and access separately.
- Neither quantum, SKR, martingale transport nor spectral theory is a mandatory connection.
  The paper identifier in the old prompt is unverified here. A sophisticated method must beat
  the classical/simple baseline on a defined task before becoming load-bearing.
- Long/short and beta-neutral portfolios can lose on both legs. Joint tails, factor exposures,
  financing, short borrow and liquidity require explicit modeling.

## 12. Separate delivery-prediction features from tradable equity features

The current main branch now has a sizable project-month hazard panel and several negative
out-of-sample tests. Those features ask whether a generator's promised date will move; they do not
measure a listed company's contemporaneous cash-flow exposure or predict its residual return. The
equity obligation panel is only 130 facts across PWR and ETN, with 28 lacking a matched acceptance
timestamp; EME and DLR are absent. The return event study has 84 events and is concentrated in PWR.
The daily price input exists for five symbols, but the event runner still fetches sector controls
live, so its full result is not a pinned reproducible feature panel.

This changes what “data features are ready” means: the team is ready to build and test a point-in-
time schema, not to claim it already has a complete equity signal. `strategy-feature-contract.md`
owns the current field contract, inventory, feature/label split and hard readiness gates. The
immediate first baseline should compare source-reviewed company disclosures against the firm's
own prior public guidance, while keeping analyst consensus explicitly unavailable until genuine
historical estimate vintages are obtained. Repeated reports must be clustered by underlying shock;
more rows are not automatically more independent evidence.

The newly started SEC raw archive run is still in progress at the time of this note. Its completion
must be evidenced by reconciled accession/document counts and content hashes before calling the
filing package archived. TigerData writes remain paused pending verified storage headroom; Snowflake
is the current raw archive/feature-panel destination, and kdb+ remains a derived HPG time-series
layer that must pass its own smoke test.
