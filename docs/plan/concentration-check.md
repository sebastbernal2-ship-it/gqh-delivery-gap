# The concentration check on the delivery tail

**Question**: does any listed firm carry a single slipped project as a material share of its economics, which is
what the delivery tail chain needs to have an undiluted instrument.

**What was done**: joined the exposure panel, 2,181 rows and 274,176 MW of slipped capacity, against the
entity to ticker crosswalk.

**What it found**: the crosswalk holds **one** evidenced row. The share of slipped capacity attributable to a
listed owner is 0.00% in the panel, and the largest slipped entities are project
companies, not issuers: Power Company of Wyoming LLC, Georgia Power Co, Invenergy Services LLC, Solar Proponent LLC.

**Conclusion**: the concentration test cannot be run at scale, and the binding artifact is the crosswalk itself
rather than more data. A mapping from project company to owner with a cited source is what turns
274,176 MW of measured slippage into a testable exposure, and that is document work over filings and
interconnection filings rather than a data purchase.
