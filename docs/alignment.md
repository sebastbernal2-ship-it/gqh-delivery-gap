# Alignment: how we think

This is the file about intuition, vision, and method. It is not a plan and not a summary of the
idea. It is the thing that keeps four people making the same kind of decision when nobody is
watching.

Owned by: (name it). Everyone edits it only by proposal, so the shared standard stays stable.

## How we decide

Answer these in the team's own words, and keep the answers short.

- When is a claim allowed to become something we trade or build on?
- What counts as evidence here, and what is not evidence even when it looks like it?
- What do we do when a result is good? (Who tries to break it, and how?)
- What do we do when a result is bad?
- What makes us abandon a line of work, and who decides?

## How we process a new input

Reading, a paper, a dataset, a teammate's draft, an idea at 2am. What is the path from "I saw this"
to "we are using this"? Name the steps. Keep them few.

## What we are trying to be right about

The one or two questions that everything else serves. If a piece of work does not serve them, it
does not go in.

## How we treat each other's work

- Criticise the claim, not the person.
- Give the reason, and the evidence that would change your mind.
- If you disagree with a decision that is already settled, reopen it in `docs/decisions.md` with the
  reason, rather than working around it.

## What we are not doing

Things we have decided are not worth the time, and why. This section saves the most hours.
