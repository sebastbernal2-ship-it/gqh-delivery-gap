## How to read this

The order of the sections is the order of force: a rule closes a position whether the owner likes it or not, a contract binds one party, capital limits what a dealer can warehouse, a procedure queues people, attention costs money to spend, and latency decides who is present. A participant whose constraint is `none` cannot be used.
