# The market map: what it is and what each record means

The node space says what we measure. The chain logs say how one claim derives from another. This map says who
the participants are, what forces them to transact or to bear, what flows that produces, which instrument carries
the flow without dilution, and which of our measured nodes maps onto it.

One record per line in `map.jsonl`. The validator is `scripts/check_market_map.py` and the view is generated by
`scripts/render_market_map.py`. Nothing here is a position. A participant with no forced flow is a participant we
cannot use.

## Record types

**`participant`**. A class of agent, not a company. Fields: `id`, `name`, `kind` (one of fund, dealer, operator,
contractor, lender, provider, index, issuer, household, venue, supplier, utility), `constraint` (one of rule,
mandate, contract, capital, procedure, attention, latency, none), `constraint_note` (what they must do and why
they cannot stop), `evidence`, `falsifier`.

**`flow`**. What the constraint produces. Fields: `id`, `participant`, `mechanism`, `direction`, `calendar`
(the clock the flow runs on), `size_note`, `instrument`, `observable` (public or private), `data_note`,
`evidence`, `falsifier`.

**`instrument`**. What we would hold. Fields: `id`, `description`, `venue`, `capacity_note`, `concentration`
(concentrated, diluted, or unknown), `node` (a node id in the declared node space) or `no_node_reason`.

**`mapping`**. Ties a measured node to a flow: `id`, `node`, `flow`, `relation` (expresses, indicates, conditions,
measures), `evidence`, `falsifier`.

## Rules the validator enforces

Every flow names a participant that exists and an instrument that exists. Every participant names a constraint
from the declared set. Every instrument names a node that exists in `docs/scan/nodes.jsonl`, or states why it has
none. Every mapping names a node and a flow that exist. Ids are unique. Every record carries evidence and a
falsifier, because a claim with no falsifier is not a claim.
