## 4. Implement the smallest deterministic research path

Normalize daily prices/actions and events; implement availability joins and numerical/accounting
tests; then a simple declared signal and cost model. L1 improves event-window execution checks.
L2/L3 samples are a separate engineering test, not a prerequisite to this economic study.

## 5. Check the mechanism and failure modes

Event clusters, regime coverage, demand/earnings explanations, factor/sector exposures,
survivorship, joint tails, short borrow, costs and doubled costs. Compare simple baselines;
record negative findings. Add advanced methods only with development-period evidence of value.

Extend comparable history and firms, version datasets and freeze parameters. Generate result
artifacts from code. Open/report the sealed test under the track protocol, without redesigning
from its outcome. Compute/perpetual/quantum extensions stay separate and clearly short-history
or experimental. Do not present engineering scalability as statistical validation.
