# Reasoning, intuition and alignment proposal

Owner: aidanq06. Complements the existing alignment proposal; not a unilateral rewrite of team policy.

## The questions behind an idea

Who must pay or receive money? Which constraint changes that payment? Relative to what earlier
expectation? When can we know? Why might the price response be incomplete? What would show that
we are merely measuring earnings news, beta or liquidity risk? Can we actually trade the exposure?

For every intuition, write the chain: observation → physical implication → contractual implication
→ expected price response → tradable decision. Mark each arrow as observed, inferred or untested.
A stronger physical story does not automatically strengthen every later arrow.

## How we process inputs

Capture the source and its original availability; identify the claim and its owner; compare it
with the current thesis; write the simplest alternative explanation; name the discriminating test.
Put unfinished reasoning in inbox, discussion in thinking, and a formal falsifiable hypothesis in
the thesis ledger only when its scope is explicit. Record adopted choices in decisions. Results
belong in generated artifacts; the note cites those artifacts rather than a model's narrative.

Maintain an experiment record for every attempted variant: hypothesis, permitted training range,
features, parameters, eligibility/exclusions, costs, seed, run identity, outcome and interpretation.
A negative experiment remains evidence. Avoid searching relations among everything merely because
feeds are available. Data feasibility may narrow a mechanism; it must not invent its justification.

## Confidence is local to a claim

Documentation-verified capability is not authenticated entitlement. A downloaded dataset is not a
valid point-in-time panel. A correct label is not a profitable forecast. A calibrated marginal
forecast does not establish correct joint tails. A passing unit test does not prove a system's
financial assumptions. State the last verified step and the next test explicitly.

Long histories matter through coverage and independent shocks, not calendar prestige. Regimes
matter through changing economics and execution, not simply labels on a histogram. Report event
and issuer counts, shared shocks, missingness and uncertainty by condition. Do not claim precise
extreme-tail probabilities from a few events, or manufacture evidence by sampling a fitted model.

## How disagreement becomes progress

Record: prior belief; objection; supporting observation; revision; retained uncertainty; next test.
For example, two years being permitted by a split formula does not mean two years proves robustness.
Correct the overstatement openly. If OOS fails during a new regime, that is a real deployment
failure to explain, not permission to relabel the sample until it passes.

## What tools earn

A component needs a named task, a simple comparator, a measurable benefit and its full cost.
Include research time, inference, encoding, transfer, calibration and execution latency when
relevant. HiPerGator resources do not compel a GPU model; quantum expressivity is not alpha.
Choose runtime/language from the task and team capability. Strict typing can prevent certain
errors but does not make floating-point arithmetic exact or economic reasoning correct.

## Writing and review

Follow docs/writing/style.md. For the note, organize around mechanism, data/timing, method,
results/uncertainty, risk/capacity and limitations. Every figure should answer a research question.
An adversarial reviewer should trace claims to sources, reproduce figures, inspect excluded events,
and ask what simpler model explains the result. Model-assisted critique is useful feedback, not
an official judge verdict. Claims about judge identities or automated grading were not verified here.
