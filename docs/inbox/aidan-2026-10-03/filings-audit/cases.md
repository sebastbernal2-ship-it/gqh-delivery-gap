## VOGTLE-4 — revision and later delivery

Scope: outside the PWR/ETN/DLR basket; illustrates a measurable mechanism. Oglethorpe is a cooperative, not a listed equity ticker. Southern Company exposure requires its own attribution.

| Stage | Evidence | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Prior expectation | [Southern Q3 2023 filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1004155/000009212223000101/so-20230930.htm), Vogtle construction section | An October 6 pump issue was followed by a Q1 2024 service expectation |
| Revision | [Oglethorpe February 1, 2024 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/788816/000162828024002916/opc-20240201.htm), Item 8.01 | Cooling-system piping vibrations and remaining tests; service expectation moved to Q2 2024 |
| Reaffirmation | [Oglethorpe year-end investor briefing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/788816/000162828024015573/oglethorpepowerq4andye20.htm), Vogtle section | Still expected in Q2; same project, not a new independent shock |
| Outcome | [Oglethorpe April 29, 2024 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/788816/000162828024018788/opc-20240429.htm), Item 8.01 | Commercial operation on April 29, within the revised quarter |

The February filing says Oglethorpe expected roughly $15 million additional financing cost per month of delay during Q2; beyond Q2, approximately $20 million monthly combined construction/financing cost. These are **Oglethorpe's** estimates, not Southern's equity loss. It separately reports that Georgia Power did not expect the extension to change its total project capital-cost forecast. Do not multiply one owner's cost rate by another owner's percentage.

Observed: component problem, project-level schedule revision, owner-specific cost estimates, later delivery. Inferred: the physical issue constrained the commissioning path. Untested: novel information at an executable timestamp, magnitude of any equity mispricing, return persistence. Q1-to-Q2 is a revision between ranges, not proof of exactly 90 days of delay. The latest pre-event baseline and earliest dissemination still require a complete intervening-document search.

## WESTERN-SPIRIT-WIND — candidate successful control

- [Initial construction announcement](https://patternenergy.com/pattern-energy-closes-financing-and-starts-full-construction-of-western-spirit-wind-projects-in-new-mexico/) targets commercial operation by end-2021. Its page header says January 4, 2021, while the body dateline says January 4, 2020. Preserve that conflict; do not invent a precise historical timestamp.
- [Transmission energization announcement](https://patternenergy.com/pattern-energy-and-reta-announce-energization-western-spirit-transmission-line-new-mexico/) reiterates wind-project completion at end-December 2021. Wind and transmission are linked but distinct assets; energization is not automatically commercial operation.
- [Pattern's 2022 sustainability report](https://patternenergy.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Sustainability-Report-2022_sprd_web.pdf), utility-scale portfolio table, identifies Western Spirit Wind's commercial-operation year as 2021. This corroborates delivery at year resolution; it supplies neither an exact completion timestamp nor proof of no intermediate revisions.
- [Quanta's SunZia announcement](https://investors.quantaservices.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/338/quanta-services-selected-for-the-sunzia-transmission-and-sunzia-wind-projects) identifies Blattner's Western Spirit wind work. Attribution to PWR must respect its 2021 acquisition of Blattner; do not conflate the wind contractor with the transmission contractor.

Label: candidate year-resolution delivery consistent with the original target. Not a certified clean on-time control until intervening revisions and original publication times are audited. Record this as one related project cluster, not several controls because multiple documents repeat it.

## SUNZIA — baseline found, revision unresolved

[Quanta's May 2023 announcement](https://investors.quantaservices.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/338/quanta-services-selected-for-the-sunzia-transmission-and-sunzia-wind-projects) describes separate wind and transmission scopes, Q4 2023 construction starts and end-2025 completion expectations. The announcement also separates limited notice to proceed from expected later inclusion of the full wind scope in backlog.

[Quanta's 2024 sustainability report, published in 2025](https://sustainability.quantaservices.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/2024-Quanta-Sustainability-Report.pdf) refers to planned commercial operations in 2026. That is outside the core disclosure window and a different milestone. **Do not label the two documents a verified delay pair.** Preserve construction completion versus commercial operation, find like-for-like updates, and resolve contractor cash-flow exposure first.

Status: original quantified promise located; no certified revision or realization in this audit. Unresolved is not unchanged, on-time or delayed.
