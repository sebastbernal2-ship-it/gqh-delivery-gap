# Candidate equity strategy and scope

Owner: aidanq06. Proposal; no claim of demonstrated alpha.

## Economic object

Promised infrastructure is not usable, billable capacity. Power, cooling, hardware, networking,
commissioning and customer acceptance can all constrain delivery. Track phases rather than assuming
an entire campus opens on one date. Sequential dependencies require a critical-path model; taking
the maximum of independent task durations is not sufficient.

Question: do new, observable revisions to usable capacity predict contract-specific cash-flow
changes and subsequent abnormal equity returns after public-news, factor exposure and costs?

A proposed reason for delayed pricing is fragmented evidence across project updates, filings and
counterparties. It is a hypothesis to test, not an assertion that investors ignore public news.
Guidance is a baseline, not necessarily market consensus. A delay already anticipated in prices
is not automatically a trade.

## Candidate trade, conditional on evidence

- New evidence revises delivery expectations for the same project and target period.
- Determine whose revenue, expenses or penalties actually change under the relevant contract.
- Candidate short: materially harmed operator. Candidate long: a compatible existing supplier
  with spare capacity, ability to serve that customer, and persistent demand.
- Do not force both legs. Skip when exposure, substitution, borrow, liquidity or timing is unclear.
- Entry follows public availability plus ingestion/processing and an executable market opportunity.
- Ten sessions was suggested as an initial horizon, not chosen from results or approved as final.
- Size and hedge with development-period estimates, liquidity limits and common-project/customer
  concentration controls. Exact thresholds and de-risk/re-entry rules remain unresolved.

Demand cancellation, fixed-price agreements, guarantees, deferred capex and absent spare capacity
can reverse the proposed signs. A supplier commitment to buy available compute need not insure
construction delays. Geographic, GPU and workload compatibility matter. Water is initially a site
constraint; gas, FX and broad commodity trades require their own material transmission mechanism.

## Two applications, separate claims

Established-market application: find historical delivery/contract surprises with comparable
financial consequences and actual contemporaneous equity exposure. Power, equipment and fab
examples are candidates; neither a twenty-year dataset nor an approved universe has been obtained.

Compute application: transfer frozen economic rules into the shorter modern sample. Test whether
compute pricing adds information on the overlap. A long industrial study does not become a long
Ornn, perp or HFT backtest. Do not select the analogue because its returns support the desired story.

Equities are the first implementation. The current team source audit proposes PWR as a pipeline
pilot; our transcript also discussed short NVDA/GOOGL market-data pilots. None is a validated
universe or selected profitable event. Preserve that distinction until an owner fixes the pilot.

## Validation layers and failure criteria

1. Facts: human-reviewed source-backed extraction, dates/units, confidence and abstention.
2. Physical forecast: improvements over prior public schedules, with cancellation distinct from delay.
3. Financial mapping: demonstrable exposure and a contemporaneous instrument.
4. Alpha: subsequent returns beyond sector/momentum/news baselines, measured after costs.
5. Execution: attainable fills, funding/borrow, capacity and delayed-entry sensitivity.

Reject or narrow when the forecast improves but prices already respond, results are factor exposure,
profits depend on one company/shock, timing assumptions drive the result, or plausible costs erase it.
Remove a failed advanced component without pretending it invalidates or validates the core mechanism.

Validation protocol is governed by docs/brief.md. Keep final chronological OOS untouched, use
chronological development folds, remove label overlap across boundaries, and group common shocks.
The regime study reports conditional performance; random balanced regime samples are not a substitute
for deployment testing. Fit regime definitions and thresholds within each historical training window.
