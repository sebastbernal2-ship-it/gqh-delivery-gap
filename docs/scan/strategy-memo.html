Thesis A: liquidity provision to forced deleveraging

Mechanism

A leveraged account on a perpetual venue is closed by the margin engine at a price and a time it does not

choose. The liquidation order is marketable, consumes displayed depth, and pushes price away from fair

value. The move overshoots in proportion to how thin the book is. Whoever supplies liquidity to that flow

is paid the overshoot, net of costs, provided the dislocation is larger than the round trip.

Why it persists: supplying capital into a cascade requires holding inventory exactly when it is

dangerous. This is a risk premium, not an information edge, so it does not close the way a mispricing

does. The barrier to entry is the willingness to hold that risk and the machinery to do it without being

the fastest participant.

Trigger specification, as frozen

The conjunction in docs/plan/cascade-protocol.md, implemented in src/live/hyperliquid.py:

  • a one sample step beyond three trailing standard deviations, the trailing window sixty samples at
  • fifteen seconds;
  • and open interest down one percent or more against the sixty sample median.

This is the only condition whose measured gross reversion clears the cost hurdle. Percentile based

conditions do not (T20, T22).

What we do not know

Whether the reversion survives outside the recorded window; whether the reversion is concentrated in the

first seconds (which would turn this into a speed business and violate our latency clause); whether venue

fees stay at current levels; whether the same conjunction fires in other venues with deeper books.

Promotion gate

At least thirty conjunction events in a tape of several days, net positive at doubled costs, permutation

null beaten, and a replay of the exact proposed rule (entry, size, exit) on the recorded tape matching the

study's numbers within one basis point. Until then this stays a candidate.

Thesis B: expectation gap in the capital intensive complex

Mechanism

A firm raises capex as a share of revenue. Intensity alone is not information: it is a spending pattern,

and under controls it does not predict relative returns (T33). The thesis becomes an expectation claim

only with a conversion or surprise measure: the firm promised backlog, guidance or revenue conversion, and

the filing shows it did not deliver. The counterparty is the holder who acted on the promise. The receiver

is whoever is positioned before the revision is fully in the tape.

Signal specification, current and planned

  • Base measure, built: year over year change in capex intensity from SEC XBRL, known at the later
  • filing date, valid 180 days.
  • Surprise measures, planned: guidance revisions from earnings exhibits (item 2.02 releases), backlog
  • conversion from RPO change against its typical seasonal change, and revenue conversion below the name's
  • own trailing four quarter norm.
  • The hypothesis to test: intensity up and a negative filing surprise interacts to predict weaker
  • relative returns. The realised conversion version of this test failed so far; guidance is the untested
  • and more faithful version.

Measured so far

  • The charge existed in the simple design: -0.29 at five days, -0.24 at twenty (T25), and it is not asset
  • growth (T29), sign stable across three regimes (T30), fragile without the disclosure names (T31).
  • Under controls it fails: +0.0061, t 0.65 with sector, growth, margin and the common factor (T33).
  • The expression layer, if the channel is later established: net 13.1 percent annualised against 15.9
  • gross, Sharpe 0.381, drawdown -65.8 percent, 231 cohorts, capacity 3.87M dollars at one percent
  • participation, 19.4M at five (T32). Unattributed until the channel test passes.

What we do not know

Whether guidance revisions carry the channel; whether the effect is concentrated in a few names; whether

borrow is available and at what cost for the short leg; whether a risk overlay can bring the drawdown to an

acceptable level.

Promotion gate

The interaction coefficient negative with t beyond two in the controlled specification, on a disjoint name

set, with borrow measured, and the strategy's risk overlay bounded.

What we will not do

  • No queue or delivery trades: no instrument isolates the units (T6, T21).
  • No options expressions without an entitlement that does not exist here.
  • No latency race: if a thesis only works in the first seconds, it is out of scope by our own rule.
  • No new variants after a sealed window opens; both are spent, so the remaining work is declared
  • development and labelled as such.

Cross cutting

  • State machine: the queue and delivery work supplies regime state (where interconnection binds, who
  • withdraws, phase of the cycle). It sizes and gates the other two, it is not a P&L chain.
  • Data: docs/scan/datasets.jsonl lists the 162 datasets with sources and coverage; the graph holds
  • them as nodes.
  • Bookkeeping: every thesis keeps a chain log (docs/chains/), every measurement becomes a truth
  • (docs/truths.md), every choice is a decision event.
  • Capital: thesis A is measured in single digit millions before depth binds; thesis B in single digit
  • millions at one percent participation. Both are small books by construction.
