## What each line tried

- **scan, mechanism window**: every declared pair, each against its own generated null. null. Reproduce with `python scripts/run_association_scan.py --out results/scan-pairs-mechanism.csv`.
- **scan, compute era**: the same scan inside the compute era window. cross-family survivors, candidates only. Reproduce with `python scripts/run_association_scan.py --out results/scan-pairs-compute-era.csv`.
- **compute price against everything else**: 11 pairs, one per other series, at monthly frequency. null, and underpowered by the window length. Reproduce with `python scripts/run_association_scan.py --window compute-era`.
- **firm disclosures, 382 companies**: four industry groups, two directions, five horizons. cells above two sigma, see the family correction. Reproduce with `python scripts/run_group_event_study.py`.
- **the same study on shuffled dates**: the placebo for the line above. cells above two sigma, see the family correction. Reproduce with `python scripts/run_group_event_study.py --placebo`.
- **firm-level revision study, two companies**: 84 timestamped revisions, both directions across 5 horizons. null on the predicted direction. Reproduce with `python scripts/run_revision_event_study.py`.
- **aggregate capacity revision strategy**: 3 signal definitions, three selectivity cuts, one robustness pair. no cell positive after costs at the doubled rate. Reproduce with `python scripts/run_capacity_strategy.py`.
- **delivery revisions, project level**: nine annual vintages, every generator. measurement, no null test. Reproduce with `python scripts/build_delivery_panel.py --years 2015-2023`.
- **promise survival**: first revision timing, with and without month-sized nudges. measurement, no null test. Reproduce with `python scripts/build_promise_survival.py`.

A survivor in a scan line is a candidate for a mechanism conversation, never a strategy, and the family correction is what separates the two.
